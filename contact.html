Frequently Asked

Essential voyage information for prospective deep sea explorers.

No scuba or prior diving certification is required. Because our submersibles maintain a 1-atmosphere internal pressure, you experience zero physical decompression. All guests complete a basic medical evaluation prior to boarding.
Our vessels feature solid titanium pressure hulls tested to 1.5x maximum depth pressure. Every vessel carries quad-redundant life support, acoustic communication drop-weights, and automatic ballast release mechanisms. We hold a 100% safety record across 250+ deep ocean dives.
All bookings include full luxury accommodations on the mother vessel, gourmet meals by onboard Michelin-rated chefs, submarine dive seats, 8K video footage of your dive, and official ABYSS Explorer certification.
